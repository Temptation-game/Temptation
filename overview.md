# TEMPTATION

*for all rules marked with \*, read it as "unless a card specifies otherwise".*

Welcome to Temptation, a tactical card game about nuns and vampires, where all sides are lesbian!

There are two factions in the game: Church and Court.

There are three types of cards: a Character, an Item, and an Incantation.

Each Character card has two sides, who they are as a nun and who they are as a vampire.

During the game, a card on the field may get flipped and be given to your opponent into their hand. Such card is called *tempted*. Sometimes a card can also be flipped without leaving the field - when you do so, you *convert* it.

Item and Incantation cards are auxiliary. You never start with these cards in your deck.

## Start of game

Each player starts with 5 cards.

On their first turn, the first player has 1 Wine/Blood and the second has 2 Wine/Blood. After that, the Wine/Blood counter proceeds as described in Turn.

## Turn

Each turn has the following structure:

1) Start phase:
- Draw a card, if possible.
- If you have more than 6 cards in hand, discard until you have 6 cards.
- Reset Wine/Blood (equal to the number of current player's turn, capped at 10 + modifiers).
2) Play phase: Play your new characters on the field.
3) Act phase: Act for your Characters (you have a shared pool of 3 actions + modifiers).

## Game end

You lose the game when at the end of your play phase, you no longer have characters on the field and you cannot or do not play a character (either because you cannot pay the cost or because there are no holy/unholy squares to place one in).

## Field

A field has *holy*, neutral, and *unholy* squares. A nun can only enter the field in a *holy* square\*. A vampire can only enter the field in an *unholy* square\*.

A square can be *consecrated* and become *holy*. It can be *desecrated* and become *unholy*.

An *adjacent* square is a square directly north, west, south, or east of a given one.

A square is *unoccupied* if there is no Character or Large item in it.

## Discard pile

A character can be discarded due to hand limit, or due to a Special. A discarded character cannot re-enter the game\*.

## Characters

To play a Character, pay her cost in Wine/Blood, then place her on the field in your faction's unoccupied square\*, your faction's side up\*. If a Character has an Icebreaker, do what it says in the Icebreaker.

Each Character has Charm and Composure. Some Characters may also have a Special or Chant specified.

As a part of your Act phase, you can have your character act (a character is yours as long as she is facing your side's up). The default actions are to *move* to an adjacent unoccupied square, *drop* or *pick up* an item in your square, or to *flirt* (both your and an opposite-faction Character in an adjacent square lose Composure). The number of Composure points a character loses equals to her interlocutor's *Charm* + modifiers.

Actions can be also spent on triggering a Special or a Chant. Some of them may cost multiple actions.

A character does not act same turn as she enters unless she is *Adventurous*.

A character is yours as long as she is facing your faction's side up. If your Character loses all Composure, she is *tempted*, and her card is given to the opponent. Next time she is played, she enters with full Composure\*.

A Character can only be *converted* through Specials. When a character is *converted*, she restores her Composure to the full value on the new side\*.

## Marriage

If both the flirting character and her target (or targets) would have been *tempted* simultaneously, they get *married*. Married characters leave the game. Unlike the discarded characters, they cannot be brought back by any means, including Chants and Specials.

Items the newly married characters were carrying go to the discard pile.

## Item

Item cards cannot be part of the starting deck; they are brought in by Character's Specials or Icebreakers.

Most Items are one-sided and belong to the specific faction. Some are neutral or two-sided. This may affect the way some Specials or Chants work.

A Character cannot flirt with an Item\*.

An Item card can be Carried by a Character or stay on a square of the field.

Some Item cards are *large*. They occupy a place on the field and cannot be carried.

A Character can only carry one Item at a time\*. An item of a Character who got tempted is discarded.

## Incantations

An Incantation card is brought into play by *chanting*. The description of the *Chant* on a Character card should include cost (in actions and Wine/Blood), placement rules (Self, Distance X, or Irrelevant), and Target rules (1 square, Distance X, or Whole field).

Some Incantations can be *delayed* (only enter after X turns) or *long* (have to be *chanted* for X turns in a row to take effect).

An Incantation vanishes immediately upon playing.

## Location

The default location is Town Square. It is a 4x4 playing field, where the westmost column is *unholy*, and the eastmost column is *holy*.
