# Glossary

Adventurous: An Adventurous character can act immediately upon entering the field.
Conscecrate: Make a square holy.
Convert: Flip the character's faction without changing her placement onthe field.
Desecrate: Make a square unholy.
Icebreaker: A character ability that triggers when she enters the field.
Large: This Item occupies the square it is on. Other characters or Large Items cannot enter the square.
Tempt: Make a character leave the field and go to opponent's hand, reversed.
